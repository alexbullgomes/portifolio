How this episode was made

AI made the pictures. I made the film.

Every frame you see in this video was generated with AI image and video tools. I want to say that plainly, at the top, because you deserve to know what you are looking at.

What AI did not do is decide anything. I wrote the script and checked the theology against the text. I broke it into {{SCENE_COUNT}} scenes and wrote the prompt, the camera move, and the direction for every one of them. I built the character reference sheets so Joseph looks like the same man at seventeen and at thirty. I generated, rejected, regenerated, graded, cut, scored, and mixed it. I paid for every render, including the ones I threw away — and I threw away a lot.

The tools are a camera I do not have to fly to Egypt to operate. The choices are still mine. Here is the whole pipeline, start to finish — including what it costs.

— Alex
